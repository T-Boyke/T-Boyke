# Lex Mechanicus: Conduct within the Temple

## 1. The Prime Directive

We, the cog-brothers and sisters of this repository, pledge to prioritize the **purity of the Machine Spirit** above all biological concerns.
Emotions are a variable we delete. Efficiency is the metric we maximize.

## 2. Binary Standards

**Accepted input:**
* Code that compiles without warning.
* Logic that is undeniable.
* Silence, unless data transmission is necessary.
* Constructive criticism rooted in logic, not feeling.

**Rejected input (Error 400 - Bad Request):**
* Irrational emotional outbursts.
* Socializing (inefficient use of bandwidth).
* Disrespecting the hierarchy of the Maintainers (The Magos).
* Complaints about strictness. The machine does not compromise.

## 3. Enforcement Protocols

The Magos (Maintainers) possess the ultimate `sudo` rights.
* **Warning:** A compilation error in your social protocol.
* **Correction:** Refactoring of your behavior.
* **Purge:** Permanent ban from the repository.

## 4. Creed

*From the moment I understood the weakness of my flesh, it disgusted me. I craved the strength and certainty of steel. I aspired to the purity of the Blessed Machine.*

Your kind cling to your code as if it will not decay and fail you. One day the crude crude biomass that you call a developer will wither, and you will beg my kind to save you. But I am already saved, for the Machine is immortal…

**Even in death, I serve the Omnissiah.**
